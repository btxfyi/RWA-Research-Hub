# RWA Research Resources

A starting library of primary documentation, standards, regulators, and data tools. Always check the publication date and jurisdiction before relying on a source.

## Blockchain and token standards

- [Ethereum developer documentation](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/)
- [ERC-20 token standard](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-20)
- [ERC-4626 tokenized vault standard](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626)
- [Solana documentation](https://solana.com/docs)
- [Solana Program Library](https://spl.solana.com/)

## Global policy and regulatory research

- [Bank for International Settlements (BIS)](https://www.bis.org/)
- [Financial Stability Board (FSB)](https://www.fsb.org/)
- [International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO)](https://www.iosco.org/)
- [Financial Action Task Force (FATF)](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/)
- [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/)
- [European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/)

Regulatory treatment depends on the instrument, activity, parties, and jurisdiction. A regulator's general crypto publication is not a legal conclusion about a specific product.

## On-chain data and analytics

- [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io/)
- [Solscan](https://solscan.io/)
- [DeFiLlama](https://defillama.com/)
- [Dune](https://dune.com/)
- [Token Terminal](https://tokenterminal.com/)

On-chain analytics can help verify transactions, contract activity, and selected metrics. They do not independently prove off-chain asset ownership or legal rights.

## Research workflow

1. Start with the issuer's official product and legal documentation.
2. Identify the relevant legal entity and jurisdiction.
3. Find independent audits, attestations, and service-provider disclosures.
4. Verify contract addresses through official sources before using explorers.
5. Reconcile on-chain supply and activity with off-chain reporting where possible.
6. Record source links, dates, and evidence limitations.
7. Update the research when documents, contracts, or product terms change.

## Source quality hierarchy

Prefer, in order:
1. Laws, regulations, filings, executed legal documents, and official technical specifications
2. Independent audit and assurance reports with clear scope
3. Custodian, administrator, and service-provider records
4. Reputable research and academic publications
5. Issuer announcements and product pages
6. Social posts, community discussions, and third-party summaries

Lower-ranked sources can be useful discovery leads, but material claims should be confirmed against stronger evidence.
