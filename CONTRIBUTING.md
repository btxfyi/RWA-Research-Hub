# Contributing to RWA Research Hub

Contributions are welcome: corrections, research notes, case studies, glossary improvements, and educational explainers.

## Before contributing

- Read the relevant material and avoid duplicating existing content.
- Prefer primary sources and include direct links.
- Add publication dates or access dates for information that can change.
- Clearly label issuer-reported claims, independent evidence, and your own analysis.
- Explain uncertainty and missing information.
- Keep language factual, accessible, and neutral.

## Suggested workflow

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a focused branch for your change.
3. Add or edit Markdown files.
4. Check links, spelling, tables, and headings.
5. Open a pull request explaining what changed and listing important sources.

## Research quality checklist

- [ ] Claims are supported by sources.
- [ ] Primary sources are preferred.
- [ ] Dates and jurisdictions are included where relevant.
- [ ] Facts are separated from opinions and estimates.
- [ ] Risks and limitations are discussed.
- [ ] No guaranteed-return or price-promotion language is used.
- [ ] No confidential, private, or personal data is included.
- [ ] The content is educational and understandable.

## File naming

Use lowercase kebab-case names, for example:
- `private-credit-case-study.md`
- `oracle-risk-notes.md`
- `tokenized-treasury-funds.md`

## Scope

This repository is an educational research project. It does not provide investment, legal, tax, or financial advice. Contributors should not present their work as an endorsement, audit, rating, or certification of a project.

By contributing, you confirm that you have the right to share the material and that your contribution does not disclose confidential information.
