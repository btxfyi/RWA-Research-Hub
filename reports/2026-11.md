# RWA ecosystem research report — November 2026
**Published:** 20 November 2026 | **Coverage:** Tokenized funds, credit, infrastructure, data and verification

## Executive summary
RWA is not one market with one risk profile. Tokenized government-securities products, institutional credit pools and fund-tokenization infrastructure solve different problems. Research must separate the legal wrapper and underlying exposure from the blockchain rails used to issue and transfer a token.

This report reviews Ondo OUSG, Centrifuge and Maple syrupUSDC as examples of three different models.

## 1. Product structure matters more than the token label
Ondo's OUSG page describes limited partnership interests in a US-domiciled 3(c)(7) fund offered under Rule 506(c). It lists a $5,000 minimum mint/redemption and portfolio exposure including BUIDL, USDC and bank deposits. The page also displays a temporary management-fee arrangement through 1 January 2027. These are issuer-published and time-sensitive terms.
**Research implication:** a Treasury-related tokenized product is not automatically direct ownership of Treasury bills. The legal documents, underlying holdings and service providers define the actual exposure.
Source: https://ondo.finance/ousg

## 2. Tokenized-fund infrastructure is modular
Centrifuge documentation describes pools, share classes, share tokens, vaults, NAV publication and investor-access controls. Its architecture uses a hub-and-spoke model for product management and multi-chain distribution, and documents ERC-4626 and ERC-7540 vault patterns.
**Research implication:** evaluate the infrastructure and each pool separately. Pools may differ in assets, managers, legal structures, eligibility and redemption mechanics.
Sources:
- https://docs.centrifuge.io/developer/protocol/overview/
- https://docs.centrifuge.io/user/concepts/pools/
- https://developer.centrifuge.io/developer/protocol/features/composability/

## 3. Institutional credit tokens need credit analysis
Maple describes syrupUSDC as a depositor share in a pool of institutional loans and publishes loan/collateral information. Maple states loans are overcollateralized, while clarifying syrupUSDC is not itself backed by USDC; its value derives from the underlying loan assets.
**Research implication:** collateralization is not a guarantee against loss. Monitor borrower concentration, collateral volatility, haircuts, custody, liquidation, maturities and withdrawal liquidity.
Sources:
- https://maple.finance/transparency/products/syrupusdc
- https://maple.finance/transparency

## 4. Data providers require measurement discipline
RWA.xyz's data catalog exposes asset-level metrics such as NAV, circulating asset value and 30-day APY for covered assets. These metrics are not interchangeable.
**Research implication:** distinguish distributed value, circulating value, NAV, TVL, market capitalization and trading volume. Record whether each figure is issuer-reported, provider-indexed or independently computed.
Source: https://app.rwa-xyz.com/catalog

## 5. What to monitor in December
- OUSG fee changes, portfolio holdings, minimums and redemption terms.
- New Centrifuge pool launches, share classes, supported chains and vault deployments.
- Maple loan concentration, collateral coverage, maturities and withdrawal terms.
- New issuer disclosures, audits, legal documents and contract upgrades.
- Differences between issuer-reported asset values and independently computed onchain values.

## 6. Open research questions
1. How consistently do issuers publish asset and liability data?
2. How quickly can holders redeem during market stress?
3. What legal protections apply if an issuer, servicer, custodian or administrator fails?
4. How are NAV disputes handled and stale prices detected?
5. Which contract privileges can materially change investor outcomes?
6. How complete are cross-chain holder and supply datasets?

## Methodology and limitations
Based on public issuer, protocol and data-provider pages checked on 20 November 2026. This is not an independent audit, legal opinion or investment recommendation. Product terms, addresses, metrics and regulation can change. Aggregate market-size figures are omitted where definitions and timestamps are not aligned.
