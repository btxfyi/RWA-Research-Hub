## Research rules

- Link to primary sources wherever possible.
- Include access or publication dates for time-sensitive claims.
- Do not copy marketing claims as established facts.
- Clearly label estimates, opinions, and unverified information.
- Do not publish personal information or confidential investor data.
- Avoid price predictions and guaranteed-return language.

*Case studies are educational research, not endorsements or investment advice.*
