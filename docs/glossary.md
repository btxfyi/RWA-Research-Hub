# RWA Glossary

Plain-language definitions of terms commonly used in tokenization and on-chain finance. Definitions are general; legal meanings can vary by jurisdiction and document.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Asset-backed | Supported by specified assets or claims; the exact legal and economic support must be examined |
| Attestation | A statement by a third party about a defined subject and scope |
| Beneficial ownership | The right to benefit from an asset, which may be distinct from registered legal title |
| Custodian | An entity responsible for safekeeping assets or records |
| Default | Failure to meet an obligation under the relevant agreement |
| Due diligence | A structured review of facts, risks, counterparties, and documentation |
| NAV | Net asset value: assets minus liabilities, calculated using a stated methodology |
| Oracle | Infrastructure that delivers external data to smart contracts |
| Proof of reserves | Evidence about specified reserves at a particular time and within a defined scope |
| Redemption | Process through which a holder exchanges a token for cash, assets, or another defined payment |
| Servicer | Entity that administers an asset, such as collecting loan payments |
| SPV | Special-purpose vehicle formed for a defined transaction or holding structure |
| Tokenization | Representing an asset, claim, or right through a blockchain-based token |
| Transfer restriction | A rule limiting who may receive or hold a token |
| Underlying asset | The off-chain asset or claim associated with a token |
| Waterfall | Contractual order in which cash flows or recoveries are allocated |
| Rehypothecation | Reuse of assets already provided as collateral, subject to applicable agreements and law |
| Duration | A measure of a fixed-income instrument's sensitivity to interest-rate changes |
| Credit risk | Risk that an obligor fails to make required payments |
| Liquidity risk | Risk that an asset cannot be sold or redeemed promptly at a reasonable price |
| Smart-contract admin key | Privileged credential that can control specified contract functions |
| Allowlist | A list of addresses permitted to interact or transfer under a contract's rules |

## Keep these distinctions clear

- **Reserve vs. solvency:** reserves are a defined pool of assets; solvency considers the broader ability to meet obligations.
- **Price vs. NAV:** a market price is what a trade may clear at; NAV is a calculated estimate of net asset value.
- **Transferability vs. liquidity:** a token may be transferable but still have few buyers.
- **Audit vs. attestation:** these have different scopes, standards, and levels of assurance.
- **Token ownership vs. asset ownership:** holding a token does not automatically mean holding legal title to the underlying asset.
